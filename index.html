Rebel Hill Lodge. Room for all twelve of you on the Grand Ridge Road.

Gather around the fire pit

Bushland and farmland views all round, brilliant sunsets, and the firewood is provided.

The lodge

It sits at one end of Shandal and Nick's 10 acre property, with about two acres to yourselves and a privacy fence between you and their homestead. Beef, sheep and dairy farms all round.

Sleeps 12
Six bedrooms: five with a queen bed, one with two singles. Black-out blinds in every room.
Bathrooms
Three.
Living
A lounge, a living room with a wood heater, and one long dining table for everyone.
Kitchen
Oven, stove, fridge, freezer, microwave, kettle, toaster and coffee. It's self-catering.
Outside
A deck around the house, the fire pit and outdoor furniture.
Supplied
Bed linen, towels and firewood. Wi-fi and a TV. Self check-in.
Parking
Room for cars and trailers, a lockable shed for motorbikes and push bikes, and a wash bay with a pressure washer and warm water.

Swipe for all six bedrooms.

The shed

It's yours to use, free: a bar, a wood heater, seating, and a TV with wi-fi and Netflix. Good for cool evenings, and for hot summer days with your favourite people.
